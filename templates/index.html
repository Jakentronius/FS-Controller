Abort Behavior

Safety Limits

Max force: any reading beyond this (either sign, 3 consecutive samples) trips a fault — force control off, all routines and jobs aborted, Z retracted — until you clear it. Set it below your load cells' rating. Job files with higher force targets are refused outright. Z floor: machine Z that no correction, probe, jog or Go-to may descend below — set it just above the build plate as the backstop for a lying force signal. A stalled sensor (no samples for 0.5 s) also trips a fault whenever force is being relied on.

Rod Change (mid-job)

When the force controller has plunged Max rod consumption mm beyond the mesh feedforward (i.e. the mechtrode has worn down that far), the job pauses at the next engaged moment: retracts, waits for you to swap rods, tares and touches off the new rod, shifts the job coordinates by the length difference, waits for spindle confirmation, then slowly re-engages to the active force target and continues. Set 0 to disable.

Surface Mesh maps platform tilt / substrate warp with the load cells

Setup: same as Auto-Tune — rigid non-spinning rod, spindle off, all axes homed, head parked a few mm above the substrate at the center of the region to map. The routine touches off a grid (default 5×5 over a 100 mm square, rectangular grids allowed) around that point and records the contact Z at each node. During force control the mesh acts as a feedforward only: as the tool traverses, Z follows the interpolated surface tilt while the live force reading stays primary and corrects the rest. The mesh delta is zeroed wherever AUTO Z engages, so it never shifts absolute touch-off.

Grid may be rectangular (max 120 points). To catch short height waves along the weld direction, go dense on that axis: e.g. 21×2 points over 100×20 mm gives 5 mm X resolution in one strip.

Auto-Tune probes the surface and measures the machine's force response

Setup: rigid non-spinning rod in the collet, parked a few mm above the substrate near the center of the build area, scale calibrated. The routine tares in free air, measures sensor noise, creeps down in small steps until it feels contact, steps in and out in fine increments to measure stiffness and backlash, pulses the Z offset to time the loop's dead time (Kp is derived from the measured lag), verifies with a short closed-loop hold at the probe force, then retracts to the start height and recommends Kp / Deadband / Max step. Keep a hand near E-STOP the first time you run it.

Upload Job

Two formats are accepted:

  • CSV — columns X, Y, Target_Force_Units, Feedrate; one straight move per row (feedrate mm/min, force in calibrated units).
  • G-code (.gcode/.nc/.g) — standard G-code annotated with comment directives handled by the job runner:
    • ;FORCE=<value> — set the Z-force target for everything that follows
    • ;WAIT_FORCE TIMEOUT=<s> — wait until force is within the deadband of the target (default timeout 60 s; job errors out + retracts on timeout)
    • ;DWELL=<s> — hold for N seconds while force control keeps regulating
    Example touch-down cycle: 
    ;FORCE=0
G1 Z45 F600          ; travel retracted
G1 X25 Y30 F600
G1 Z10 F300          ; park just above surface
;FORCE=800
;WAIT_FORCE TIMEOUT=30
;DWELL=10
;FORCE=0
G91
G1 Z5 F600           ; lift off
G90
    Consecutive moves are motion-planned together by Klipper, so paths stay smooth. The accumulated force-control Z offset is cleared automatically when the job ends.
